Hi! I'm Carlos and share reflections on stuff as it happens.

## Background

My background is in Economics, and I've dedicated most of my work life to "financial inclusion" (essentially working for that part of the finance sector actually interested in giving access to financial services to those who typically are deemed "too risky" to be funded). These days, I'm focusing more on learning how to write maintainable software. This blog is where I try to make sense of ideas that seem easier to understand while writing about them.
